Ready to become a Healthy Food Hero?

📋 How to Play

🍎
Healthy foods: +10 points

Catch fruits, vegetables and other nutritious foods. Each healthy catch increases your combo.

🌟
Golden apple: +25 points

Catch the golden apple to earn bonus points and gain three extra seconds.

🍩
Treats: lose one life

Avoid these items in the game to keep your lives. Treats can still be enjoyed sometimes as part of a balanced eating pattern.

🔥
Combo bonus: +20 extra points

Catch five healthy foods in a row to earn a bonus.

⏱️
Beat the timer!

You have 30 seconds. Try to beat your previous high score.

💡 Healthy Food Tip

Healthy eating is about enjoying a variety of foods, balanced portions and good habits—not labelling any single food as always bad.
